- **Measured fact:** Native scored higher on model-choice (0.85 vs 0.55). Alexandria's deficit splits

## 6. Interpretation

- **H1 (token): supported, strongest of the 20-flavors.** ~3.4× cheaper median. Sparse (unrelated)
  skills give native more distinct descriptions to carry resident, inflating its cost, while
  Alexandria fetches only the one matched skill.
- **H2 (latency): supported direction, immaterial magnitude.**
- **H3 (accuracy): native wins on sparse.** When skills are unrelated and resident descriptions are
  easy to tell apart, native's always-resident catalog beats FTS. Alexandria's 3 FTS empty-results
  are the retrieval cost of lexical matching on verbose queries — the real recall weakness, distinct
  from the compliance noise.

## 7. Threats to validity

- **nrep=1** — compliance noise not averaged (6 of 9 alex misses are non-compliance).
- Native accuracy/token modeled, not scraped (conservative for H1).
- Tokenizer proxy; single machine/model/client/subset.
- Some gold labels are weak (`templates`) — auto-derived task from a thin description.
