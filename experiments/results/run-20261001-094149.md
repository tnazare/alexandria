## 5. Accuracy (H3) — STABILIZED

| N | Cond | Model-choice (rate [CI]) | Recall@5 (rate [CI]) | Consult compliance |
|----|------|--------------------------|----------------------|--------------------|
| 20 | alexandria | **0.733 [0.617–0.833]** (44/60) | 0.957 [0.89–1.0] (44/46) | 0.767 (46/60) |
| 20 | native     | **0.733 [0.617–0.850]** (44/60) | n/a | 0.850 (51/60) |

- **Model-choice is now a dead tie: 0.733 vs 0.733**, with overlapping CIs.
- At nrep=1 this flavor read 0.70 vs 0.85 (native ahead). Averaging over 3 reps closed the gap
  entirely — confirming the nrep=1 difference was LLM non-determinism, not retrieval quality.
- **8 of 20 tasks flipped their consult decision across the 3 reps** — direct evidence that
  single-rep consult-compliance is noisy and should not be read as a system difference.
- Conditioned on consulting, Alexandria recall@5 = 0.957 (44/46) — retrieval is strong when invoked.

- **Measured fact:** With nrep=3, Alexandria and native are statistically indistinguishable on
  model-choice accuracy (0.733 each) for a close-semantics library. Alexandria still trails on
  consult-compliance (0.767 vs 0.850), which is a prompt-adherence effect, not retrieval.

## 6. Interpretation

- **H1 (token): not supported on close-semantics.** No advantage; medians within noise, native even
  slightly lower. The token win requires library diversity (see sparse-20: ~3.4×), which a
  close-clustered library lacks.
- **H2 (latency): supported direction, immaterial magnitude.**
- **H3 (accuracy): tie after stabilization.** The nrep=1 "native wins" result for this flavor does
  not survive repetition. On close-semantics the systems are equivalent on model-choice; Alexandria's
  only residual deficit is consult-compliance.

## 7. Threats to validity

- nrep=3 reduces but does not eliminate compliance noise; 8/20 tasks still flipped consult decisions.
  Larger nrep would tighten further.
- Native accuracy/token modeled, not scraped (conservative for H1).
- Tokenizer proxy; single machine/model/client/subset.
- random-20 and sparse-20 remain at nrep=1 — their accuracy numbers are still compliance-contaminated
  and should be re-run at nrep≥3 when quota allows before cross-flavor accuracy claims are made.
