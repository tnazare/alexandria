## 3. Token cost (H1)

| N (subset, mode) | Alexandria (median) | Native (median) | Ratio native/alx |
|------------------|---------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| 20 (random, hybrid) | 200.0 | 1703.0 | ~8.52× |
| 20 (random, fts) [baseline] | 343.0 | 1680.0 | ~4.90× |

- Mean: alexandria 2911.0, native 2581.3. IQR alexandria [200.0–4856.3], native [607.0–3200.0].
- **Measured fact:** Alexandria's *median* cost is the ~200-token resolve floor — because on random-20
  the model often did not consult (consult rate 0.483), so the median cell paid only the round-trip.
  When it did consult-and-fetch, cost rose into the thousands (mean 2911 ≫ median 200). The wide
  median ratio (~8.5×) reflects the many no-fetch cells, not a per-query token win.

## 5. Accuracy (H3)

| N | Cond | Model-choice (rate [CI]) | Recall@5 (rate [CI]) | Consult compliance |
|----|------|--------------------------|----------------------|--------------------|
| 20 | alexandria (hybrid) | 0.483 [0.350–0.600] (29/60) | **1.000 [1.0–1.0]** (29/29) | 0.483 (29/60) |
| 20 | native | 0.717 [0.600–0.833] (43/60) | n/a | 0.717 (43/60) |
| 20 | alexandria (fts) [baseline] | 0.483 [0.367–0.600] (29/60) | 0.839 (26/31) | 0.517 (31/60) |

- **Conditional recall@5 = 1.000 (29/29):** every consult surfaced the gold skill in top-5 — up from
  FTS 0.839 on the same subset. The semantic leg recovered the ~16% of consults FTS ranked outside
  top-5 on random phrasing.
- **Model-choice 0.483 vs native 0.717:** unchanged from the FTS baseline's 0.483 — because
  model-choice here is gated by consult compliance (0.483), which hybrid cannot influence. Retrieval
  being perfect when invoked did not move the as-selected score, since the misses are non-consults.
- **Measured fact:** Hybrid lifts conditional recall to a perfect 1.000 (vs 0.839 FTS) while
  model-choice stays at 0.483 — identical to FTS — because the deficit is consult non-compliance, not
  retrieval. Native leads on model-choice via its higher effective consult rate.

## 6. Interpretation

- **H1 (token): median win inflated by non-consults; mean ~tied.** The ~8.5× median ratio is an
  artifact of the many ~200-token no-fetch cells; by mean (2911 vs 2581) the conditions are close.
- **H2 (latency): supported direction, immaterial magnitude.**
- **H3 (accuracy): hybrid delivers perfect conditional recall; as-selected unchanged.** The semantic
  leg closes FTS's random-phrasing recall gap (0.839 → 1.000), but model-choice is capped by consult
  compliance (0.483), so the headline as-selected number matches FTS exactly and trails native. The
  win is real but lives in the conditional-recall metric, not model-choice, on this subset.

## 8. Threats to validity

- Model-choice is consult-capped on random-20 (compliance 0.483); the retrieval improvement does not
  surface in as-selected accuracy because the misses are non-consults, not bad rankings.
- **Local embedding provider** (hashing/n-gram), not a production sentence model; recall gains may
  differ with a stronger embedder via the HTTP provider (no code change).
- Native accuracy/token modeled, not scraped. Tokenizer proxy; single machine/model/client; N=20.
