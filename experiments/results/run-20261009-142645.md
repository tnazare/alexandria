## 3. Interpretation

The real product resource reproduces — and slightly exceeds — the simulated catalog win on the sparse
subset. Combined with `run-20261009-132631.md` (random-20, +0.13), the catalog's advantage over native
holds on two of the three 20-skill subsets against the real backend.
