## 5. Accuracy (H3) — READ WITH THE COMPLIANCE CAVEAT

| N | Cond | Model-choice (rate [CI]) | Recall@5 (rate [CI]) | Consult compliance |
|----|------|--------------------------|----------------------|--------------------|
| 20 | alexandria | 0.45 [0.25–0.65] (9/20)  | 0.909 [0.73–1.0] (10/11) | 0.55 (11/20) |
| 20 | native     | 0.70 [0.50–0.90] (14/20) | n/a | 0.70 (14/20) |

**Compliance dominates this run's accuracy.** Of Alexandria's 11 misses, **9 were `consulted=False`**
(the model never called the resolver). Of native's 6 misses, **all 6 were `consulted=False`**.
Six tasks (t003, t008, t009, t010, t015, t016) were non-compliant in **both** conditions — the same
task phrasings suppressed tool use regardless of system, which identifies this as an LLM
prompt-adherence effect, not a retrieval difference.

When conditioned on actually consulting:
- Alexandria recall@5 = 0.909; its only two consult-but-miss tasks were t001 (correct candidate
  returned, model chose None) and t011 (empty FTS result).

- **Measured fact:** The headline 0.45 vs 0.70 gap is driven by consult-compliance failures, not
  retrieval quality. Alexandria's retrieval, when invoked, had recall@5 = 0.909.

## 6. Interpretation

- **H1 (token): supported, modest.** ~2.7× cheaper median — the advantage exists on a random mix but
  is nowhere near the full-corpus 12×.
- **H2 (latency): supported direction, immaterial magnitude.**
- **H3 (accuracy): inconclusive this run.** Non-compliance (haiku skipping the tool on certain task
  phrasings) swamps the signal. Needs nrep≥3 to average out.

## 7. Threats to validity

- **nrep=1** — single observation; compliance noise not averaged. This run is the clearest example of
  that threat materializing: 6 shared non-compliant tasks across both conditions.
- Native accuracy/token modeled, not scraped (conservative for H1).
- Tokenizer proxy; single machine/model/client/subset. No cross-flavor claims from one run.
