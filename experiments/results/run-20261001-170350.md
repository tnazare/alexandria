## 3. Token cost (H1)

| N (subset) | Alexandria (median) | Native (median) | Ratio native/alx |
|------------|---------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| 20 (random, nrep=3) | 343.0 | 1680.0 | ~4.90× |

- Mean: alexandria 1868.4, native 2514.1.
- IQR: alexandria [200.0–2392.5], native [607.0–3039.0].
- The alexandria median (343) sits near the ~200-token no-candidate floor because on `random-20`
  many tasks returned no FTS candidate (`cand=0`) and cost only the resolve round-trip; when a skill
  *was* matched and fetched, cost rose into the thousands (hence mean 1868 ≫ median 343).
- **Measured fact:** On a uniform-random 20-skill library Alexandria's median context cost is ~4.9×
  lower than native. The median gap is wide here precisely because the many empty-result tasks cost
  Alexandria almost nothing, while native always carries all 20 resident descriptions.

## 5. Accuracy (H3) — STABILIZED

| N | Cond | Model-choice (rate [CI]) | Recall@5 (rate [CI]) | Consult compliance |
|----|------|--------------------------|----------------------|--------------------|
| 20 | alexandria | **0.483 [0.367–0.600]** (29/60) | 0.839 [0.710–0.968] (26/31) | 0.517 (31/60) |
| 20 | native     | **0.700 [0.583–0.817]** (42/60) | n/a | 0.700 (42/60) |

- **Native leads on model-choice: 0.700 vs 0.483**, CIs only marginally overlapping.
- Alexandria's deficit is dominated by **consult-compliance**: it consulted on only 31/60 cells
  (0.517) vs native's 42/60 (0.700). Conditioned on actually consulting, Alexandria's recall@5 is
  strong at **0.839 (26/31)** — i.e., when the model invokes resolve, FTS usually surfaces the gold
  skill in the top 5.
- The low alexandria consult rate is visible in the run log: many `random-20` tasks show
  `consulted`-equivalent `cand=0 / chosen=None` repeated across all 3 reps (t003, t008, t009, t010,
  t013, t015, t016), i.e. the model either didn't call resolve or resolve returned nothing on these
  natural-phrasing tasks.
- **Measured fact:** With nrep=3, native outperforms Alexandria on model-choice (0.700 vs 0.483) on a
  random library, but the gap is a consult-compliance effect — Alexandria's conditional retrieval
  quality (recall@5 = 0.839) remains high.

## 6. Interpretation

- **H1 (token): supported.** ~4.9× cheaper median on a random library. The many empty-result tasks
  cost Alexandria only the resolve round-trip, while native pays the full resident-catalog cost every
  time; the mean gap (1868 vs 2514) is narrower than the median gap because matched-and-fetched tasks
  are expensive for Alexandria.
- **H2 (latency): supported direction, immaterial magnitude.**
- **H3 (accuracy): native wins on random, via compliance.** Native's always-resident catalog gives it
  a higher effective consult rate; Alexandria's lower model-choice score tracks its lower consult
  compliance (0.517), not retrieval quality — recall@5 conditioned on consult is 0.839. This mirrors
  the sparse-20 pattern (native ahead when consult noise is in play) and contrasts with
  close-semantics-20, where the two tied at 0.733 after stabilization.

## 7. Threats to validity

- nrep=3 reduces but does not eliminate consult-compliance noise; Alexandria's 0.517 consult rate is
  the dominant driver of its model-choice deficit and would benefit from larger nrep and/or a
  compliance-controlled accuracy metric.
- Native accuracy/token modeled, not scraped (conservative for H1).
- Tokenizer proxy; single machine/model/client/subset.
- Stack was freshly recreated for this run (clean DB, dev user promoted to Org_Admin); retrieval
  latency read ~82 ms vs ~47 ms on earlier runs — environmental, immaterial to the comparison.
